Public Health · Deployed Web App

CholDSS — Global Cholera Risk Early-Warning Platform

A web platform that turns remotely sensed environmental variables into cholera risk maps, with 4-week advance forecasts. It produces 1 km × 1 km pixel-level and district-scale risk surfaces across 195 countries. Building it cut a 6-hour manual workflow to under 3 minutes.

  • Automated satellite data retrieval, processing and risk calculation, so a user picks a region and gets a risk map without manual steps.
  • Built on Bayesian and statistical models of cholera transmission driven by satellite precipitation, temperature and related variables.
  • Manuscript under review at International Journal of Health Geographics.
6 hours → under 3 minutes 195 countries 1 km × 1 km resolution 4-week advance forecasts

Python · Streamlit · Leaflet.js · Google Earth Engine · Bayesian modeling

Air Quality · Current Work

Hourly Air-Quality Prediction Across the US

Spatiotemporal machine learning models that predict hourly air quality across the contiguous US by fusing satellite, reanalysis, chemical-transport and monitoring data.

  • Pipelines in Google Earth Engine and Microsoft Planetary Computer combining Sentinel-1/2, MODIS, VIIRS, ERA5, HRRR, GEOS-CF, emission inventories and EPA/TCEQ monitors.
  • Physics-based plume modeling from emission inventories as model features.
  • Leakage-free spatial, temporal and random cross-validation, Optuna tuning, SHAP interpretation and ensembling.
Hourly resolution Contiguous US Spatial + temporal CV

PyTorch · LightGBM · XGBoost · TabPFN · Temporal Fusion Transformer · Optuna · SHAP

Cloud Detection · Geostationary

Global Cloud Detection Across 6 Geostationary Satellites

Automated cloud detection pipeline covering the full Earth disk with data from GOES-18, GOES-19, Himawari-8, Himawari-9, MSG-2 and MTG-I1. It estimates sub-pixel fractional cloud coverage with Shapely polygon intersection and exports weighted cloud statistics as GeoJSON.

  • Rebuilt SEVIRI coordinates from raw GRIB navigation keys using the WMO GDT 3.90 inverse geostationary projection, handling scanning-mode differences between EUMETSAT prime and Indian Ocean (IODC) services.
  • Restored sub-kilometer geolocation for MSG-2 Indian Ocean monitoring after a GRIB2 coordinate extraction failure.
6 geostationary satellites Full-disk global coverage Sub-km geolocation

Python · Satpy · GDAL · Shapely · EUMETSAT APIs · GeoJSON · Concurrent processing

Atmospheric Science · WRF

Methane Plume Detection & Quantification

  • Scaled WRF methane plume simulations from 5 to 36,750 synthetic plumes, giving enough training data for statistically robust algorithm development.
  • Diagnosed and fixed mass-balance inconsistencies across the WRF pipeline that had been propagating into earlier emission estimates.
  • Defined the theoretical maximum replenishment rate (TMRR) for the system and built a scikit-learn model estimating instantaneous plume flow rates from atmospheric parameters.
  • Built a CSDNet cloud-segmentation pre-filter and an OpenCV feature extraction workflow, speeding up plume processing by 30%; results tracked in Weights & Biases.
5 → 36,750 plumes R > 0.6 validation 30% faster processing

Python · WRF-ARW · OpenCV · CSDNet · scikit-learn · HDF5 · Weights & Biases

Deep Learning · Oceanography

Global Vibrio Habitat Suitability — TabNet & XGBoost

Models predicting where pathogenic Vibrio bacteria are likely to thrive in coastal waters worldwide, using 11 satellite-derived oceanographic variables.

  • TabNet deep learning model across 350,506 spatial locations: 94% accuracy, AUC 0.943.
  • XGBoost global suitability map for summer 2025 across 450,786 spatial points: 84.7% accuracy.
  • Processed 10 TB+ of MODIS, GPM, MERRA-2, PACE and TRMM data into analysis-ready inputs.
AUC 0.943 450,786 spatial points 11 ocean variables

Python · TabNet · XGBoost · Google Earth Engine · Xarray · MERRA-2 · PACE
